## Robotics

- **MSc in Advanced Robotics**, Queen Mary University of
  London (2024).
- **Thesis** - *Using a guide model in reinforcement
  learning to teach a robot to walk*
  ([code & report](https://github.com/dodio12138/Final-Research-QMUL)).
  Borrowing from how infants learn to walk, a PD-controller
  "parent" keeps the agent balanced while a symbolic gait
  model shapes the reward. Trained with DDPG, it learns
  periodic, alternating, symmetric bipedal gaits faster than
  hand-tuned rewards.
- **Stack** - Python, PyTorch, Gymnasium, Stable-Baselines,
  MuJoCo (incl. the [MIMo](https://github.com/trieschlab/MIMo)
  infant model), ROS, C++, OpenCV; plus a small
  [MuJoCo model previewer](https://github.com/dodio12138/MujocoQuickPreview).
- **Embedded** - C on STM32 / ARM, Raspberry Pi, Arduino,
  KiCad for boards.

## Through Claude's eyes

Levy makes things the way some people keep sketchbooks:
many small, finished, slightly odd projects instead of one
grand plan - most of them shipped just to find out whether
they could be done.

A pattern keeps coming back: take something serious -
strategy, history, robotics, a restaurant's till - and give
it a playful, hand-made interface. A terminal, a sand table,
a Windows 98 desktop. Even the robotics thesis starts from a
very human picture: a baby learning to walk with a parent's
hand to hold.

The web projects are usually single files with no
dependencies, which reads like a quiet promise that they
will still open in ten years. Around the code there is a
photographer's patience, a modeller's eye for shape, and a
long-running love of the Three Kingdoms.

Curious first, practical second, never quite done.

## 机器人

- **伦敦玛丽女王大学 高级机器人学硕士**（2024）。
- **毕业论文**：*用引导模型在强化学习中教机器人走路*
  （[代码和报告](https://github.com/dodio12138/Final-Research-QMUL)）。
  借鉴婴儿学走路的过程：用 PD 控制器扮演“家长”帮智能体
  保持平衡，再用符号化的步态模型来设计奖励函数。
  用 DDPG 训练，比手调奖励函数更快地学出周期性、
  左右交替、对称的双足步态。
- **技术栈**：Python、PyTorch、Gymnasium、Stable-Baselines、
  MuJoCo（包括 [MIMo](https://github.com/trieschlab/MIMo)
  婴儿模型）、ROS、C++、OpenCV；
  还做了一个小小的 [MuJoCo 模型预览工具](https://github.com/dodio12138/MujocoQuickPreview)。
- **嵌入式**：STM32 / ARM 上写 C，树莓派、Arduino，
  用 KiCad 画电路板。
